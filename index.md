### **My reflection on using markdown language for cv writing**

While preparing for internship applications, I moved away from using a standard document-based CV because it did not fully demonstrate my technical identity or familiarity with developer ecosystems like GitHub. The situation required me to present not only my education level, technical skills, and objectives, but also my ability to structure information in a professional and developer-friendly format. My task was to create a CV that clearly communicated my technical background, showcased projects I had worked on in the past, and highlighted my readiness for the internship recruitment process.

To achieve this, I used Markdown language to build a clean, structured, and version-controlled CV. I researched Markdown syntax and organised sections such as my education, career objectives, technical skills, and project experience into a readable and professional layout. I also included technologies I had worked with, such as PHP, React, and Java, while linking projects directly through GitHub. This process improved both my technical documentation skills and my understanding of how developers present themselves professionally online. As a result, I successfully created a live-hosted, market-ready CV that reflected both my technical abilities and my professional growth.


### **My reflection on the mock-interview**

As part of my preparation for future technical internship interviews, I participated in and recorded multiple mock interview sessions to evaluate both my communication style and professional presentation. The situation highlighted that technical knowledge alone is not enough during interviews; employers also assess confidence, clarity, body language, and the ability to explain ideas effectively under pressure. My objective was to improve the way I communicated my technical skills, educational background, and experiences while maintaining a professional and confident presence throughout the interview process.

During the preparation process, I reviewed recordings of my mock interviews and carefully analysed my verbal and non-verbal communication habits. I noticed several areas that needed improvement, such as speaking too quickly when explaining technical concepts, limited eye contact, nervous hand movements, and occasionally sounding uncertain when answering difficult questions. To address these issues, I practiced answering common technical and behavioural interview questions repeatedly while focusing on slowing down my speech, improving my tone of voice, and maintaining better posture and hand placement. I also worked on structuring my answers more clearly using the STAR method so that my responses sounded organised, relevant, and professional.

Through continuous practice and self-evaluation, I became more comfortable presenting myself in a formal interview environment. My communication style improved significantly, allowing me to explain technical concepts in a calmer and more confident manner. The experience also helped me understand the importance of preparation, self-awareness, and professionalism in interview settings. Overall, the mock interview process strengthened both my confidence and my ability to present myself effectively as a future IT professional.


### **My reflection on using github pages**

I needed a reliable and accessible way to host both my projects and CV while also demonstrating my understanding of version control and modern development workflows. The situation required me to create a professional online presence that could easily be shared with recruiters and potential employers. My objective was to deploy a live, public-facing website directly from my source code without relying on expensive third-party hosting services or manually sending attached documents through email.

To achieve this, I researched and implemented GitHub Pages by configuring deployment settings directly within my GitHub repository. I learned how repositories, commits, and branches work together in a real-world deployment process and used this knowledge to publish my CV and portfolio online. This allowed me to manage updates efficiently while ensuring my projects remained accessible at all times. Additionally, hosting my work through GitHub Pages demonstrated my ability to use developer tools commonly used in the software industry.

As a result, I successfully created a professional online portfolio that could easily be shared through a simple web link. This not only simplified the process of sharing my CV with recruiters but also strengthened my understanding of web hosting, version control, and deployment practices. The experience improved both my technical confidence and my ability to present myself professionally in the technology field
